# License decision pending

No project license is selected by this repository change. Adding a license
text without the owner's decision could grant rights that have not been
approved.

Before publishing a `LICENSE` file, the project owner should record:

- the selected SPDX identifier and whether it covers code, data, models and
  media separately;
- third-party notices and upstream terms for the ECB+ corpus, source catalogs,
  model artifacts, fonts, images, audio and documentation assets;
- any excluded or owner-restricted material and the redistribution policy;
- an approver and decision date.

Until then, contributors must preserve existing notices, avoid adding
unreviewed third-party assets, and treat provenance/license fields in data and
media manifests as required review questions rather than permission to
redistribute.
